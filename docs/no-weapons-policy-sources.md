## Legal nuance and related statute

- Law Development Commission opinion 945/2566, pages 4–5: <https://comment.ocs.go.th/main/download/1699413535/pdf/0945-2566_ความเห็นฉบับเต็ม>. Component classification under section 4 depends on ministerial regulation. The page does not assert that no regulation exists today, or that every accessory is controlled or uncontrolled. Other laws may apply.
- Department of Provincial Administration publication of the Firearms Act B.E. 2490: <https://mis5.dopa.go.th/law4/admin/uploads/20250127125447พระราชบัญญัติอาวุธปืน%202490.pdf>. Section 24 concerns listed firearms/ammunition activities **for trade**, subject to a local registrar's licence. Preserve that qualifier.
- Later amendments and regulations must be read with the original Act; the page makes no claim of an exhaustive consolidated-law review. In particular, do not use the original PDF's section 60 corporate-liability text as current wording: that section was amended in 2560.
